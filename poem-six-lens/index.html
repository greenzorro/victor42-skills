六合读诗

Poem Six-Lens

当你想真正读懂一首古诗，它按六个维度写一篇有出处的完整解读。

When you want to actually understand a classical Chinese poem, it writes one full reading along six lenses, with sources.

能力

读出来是什么样

  • 交付物是一份完整文档：原文 → 六维解读 → 六合收敛 → 收敛时间线（表格 + 交汇反应 + 系年注）
  • 例：杜牧《过华清宫》的【世】——「写盛唐是为了诊断晚唐：距安史之乱七十年，宦官掌神策军、藩镇形同独立。诗的政治锋芒就在这古今同构里。」
  • 不是一行翻译，也不是把现成赏析换个说法

六个维度各做什么

  • 【世】时代：这首诗何以在此刻写成
  • 【人】作者：什么处境与身份让它成为必然
  • 【地】地理：地名与空间如何落地成意象
  • 【统】传统：它继承或改写了什么
  • 【受】接受史：后人怎么读它，为什么传下来
  • 【文】本体：技艺与结构——它为何是艺术

它靠什么保证不出错

  • 色温先对齐学界主流读法：自己的解读与有据主流相悖时，改的是解读，不是硬拗
  • 生卒、系年、字号、官职这类主张要定位到一手史料；查不到就写成假说，有多说就列多说
  • 平仄、韵部、体裁按平水韵核查，不靠猜

什么时候不用

  • 现代自由诗、只是想要一句翻译、作者不可考或资料极稀的篇目
  • 想要一段短评或金句，而不是一篇完整解读

Capabilities

What a reading looks like

  • One complete document: the poem → six-lens reading → convergence → a convergence timeline (table, cross-currents, dating notes)
  • For Du Mu's *Passing Huaqing Palace*, the era lens reads the High Tang scene as a diagnosis of the late Tang — seventy years after An Lushan, eunuchs holding the palace army, warlords running their own provinces; the political edge sits in that mirroring
  • Not a line-by-line translation, and not an existing essay reworded

What each lens does

  • Era — why this poem could only be written at this moment
  • Author — which circumstances and identity made it inevitable
  • Geography — how place names and space become imagery
  • Tradition — what it inherits or rewrites
  • Reception — how later readers took it, and why it survived
  • Craft — technique and structure: why it is art

Why it holds up

  • Emotional temperature is checked against the scholarly mainstream first; if the reading conflicts with a documented consensus, the reading changes, not the consensus
  • Dates, ages, courtesy names and offices must be traced to a primary source; without one the claim is written as a hypothesis, and competing accounts are listed
  • Tonal patterns, rhyme categories and form are checked against the Pingshui rhyme tables, not guessed

When not to use it

  • Modern free verse, a plain translation, or a poem whose author and dating are unknown or thinly documented
  • You want a short verdict or a quotable line, not a full reading
