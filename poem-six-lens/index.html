六合读诗

Poem Six-Lens

古典诗词深度解读方法：从时代、作者、地理、文学性、传统、接受史六个维度立体读一首诗。

In-depth classical Chinese poetry interpretation from six lenses: era, author, geography, craft, tradition, and reception history.

能力

六维读法

  • 【世】时代背景：这首诗何以在此刻写成
  • 【人】作者生平：诗人的处境与意图
  • 【地】地理：地名、空间与意象如何落地
  • 【统】文学传统：它继承或改写了什么
  • 【受】接受史：后人如何读它、为什么传下来
  • 【文】作品本体：技艺与结构——它为何是艺术

产出形态

  • 出版级深度解读，而非一行翻译或浅层赏析
  • 先写六合草稿，再收敛创作前时间线，最后合成可读文本
  • 服务对象：有好奇心、无专业文学素养的深度读者

适用与否

  • 适用：古典诗歌历史名篇（作者、系年、接受史有据可查）
  • 不适用：简单翻译、现代自由诗、作者不可考或信息极度稀薄的篇目

Capabilities

Six lenses

  • Era: why this poem was written in this moment
  • Author: the poet's situation and intent
  • Geography: places, space, and how imagery lands
  • Tradition: what it inherits or rewrites
  • Reception: how later readers shaped its afterlife
  • Craft: structure and technique — why it is art

What you get

  • Publication-grade interpretation, not a one-line gloss
  • Draft across six lenses → converge pre-composition timeline → readable synthesis
  • For curious deep readers without specialist literary training

Fit

  • Best for well-documented classical poems
  • Not for simple translation, modern free verse, or barely attested texts
