国内旅行数据源

China Travel Data

国内出行的四类数据源：航班、火车、天气、地点（高德、飞常准、携程、Open-Meteo）。写明参数格式与会让人拿到错答案的坑，另附旅行的两种模式与规划顺序。只给数据与顺序，不排行程。

Four classes of China travel data — flights, trains, weather, places (Amap, Variflight, Ctrip, Open-Meteo) — with the parameter formats and the pitfalls that quietly produce wrong answers, plus two travel modes and the planning order. Data and order only, no itinerary.

能力

能查到什么

  • 航班：班次、中转方案、票价区间
  • 火车：车次、历时、余票、票价
  • 天气：未来预报、历史同期
  • 地点：地址↔坐标、周边地点、驾车/步行/公交路线
  • 高德与飞常准需要自备 Key，携程与 Open-Meteo 开箱即用

能少踩的坑

  • 景点坐标要按地名检索，地址解析只能到城市与门牌级
  • 查航班用机场三字码，查中转与票价用城市三字码
  • 航班列表不按时刻排序，「最早/最晚」要自己排
  • 报错提示不可全信：它让你补的那个请求头，服务端根本不读

规划地基

  • 游览与度假两套公式，决定整份计划的重心
  • 两条规划顺序：先锁不可逆的交通与天数，再定可改的景点与餐厅
  • 只到模式与顺序，不给景点筛选与小时级日程

边界

  • 不抓大众点评 / 小红书：无公开 API，风控严格
  • 不共享 Key、不绕过验证码
  • 数据可以给全，结论由人下

Capabilities

What you can look up

  • Flights: schedules, transfer options, fare ranges
  • Trains: services, duration, availability, fares
  • Weather: forecast, same period in past years
  • Places: address ↔ coordinates, nearby places, driving/walking/transit routes
  • Amap and Variflight need your own key; Ctrip and Open-Meteo work as-is

Pitfalls it saves you from

  • Attraction coordinates need a place-name search; address parsing stops at city and door level
  • Look up flights by airport code, transfers and fares by city code
  • Flight lists are not time-sorted — sort them before answering first or last
  • Error messages are not gospel: the header they tell you to add is never read by the server

Planning basis

  • Two formulas — sightseeing vs vacation — that set the whole plan's centre of gravity
  • Two planning orders: lock irreversible transport and days first, then moveable attractions and meals
  • Modes and order only; no attraction shortlisting, no hour-level schedule

Boundaries

  • No scraping Dianping / Xiaohongshu: no public API, strict anti-bot
  • No key sharing, no captcha bypass
  • Data can be complete; the conclusion stays with the human
