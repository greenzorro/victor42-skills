认知塑形

Cognition Shaper

当你涉足一个不熟悉的领域，你随性提问，它一边回答一边为你建立和维护认知框架，沉淀成你的认知财产。

When you step into an unfamiliar field, you ask as things occur to you; it answers while building and maintaining the framework, so the understanding becomes something you own.

能力

对话长什么样

  • 你说：「看到新闻说芯片出口又被限制，显卡跟着涨价，想搞懂这条链」
  • 侧边框架原位长出骨架——
  • 物理底座：先进制程要用极紫外光刻，光源、镜片、光刻胶的供应商就那么几家
  • 产业层：设计 → 代工 → 封装 → 渠道，每一段的产能与库存周期都不一样
  • 制度层：出口管制清单按算力密度划线，不按产品名
  • 待验证：这轮涨价是产能不够，还是渠道惜售？看新旧卡是否同涨、交期是否同步拉长
  • 它只回一两句：「新卡和旧卡是一起涨，还是只有新卡涨？只有新卡涨指向产能与清单，全都涨更像渠道在囤。」
  • 侧边那份文档你看得见：原生侧边栏、你指定的本地 Markdown，或云端一个未公开列出的 Gist

什么时候值得开

  • 要弄懂一个陌生领域或一件新事物，而不是查一条事实
  • 一件事反复想不透，需要被一层层问下去
  • 要跟 AI 多轮共建一个理解，而不是问一句拿一个答案
  • 想把过程中的假设、反例、已确认细节留下来
  • 只有你明确要求时才启动，普通提问不会变成大工程

它跟普通聊天差在哪

  • 聊天保持短促：每轮最多推进一个因果问题，不甩一大段综述
  • 侧边文档原位展开：不写流水账，已确认的细节与未定的猜测分开放，不丢
  • 从具体现象切入，向下追到第一性原理，并标出跨层的因果

边界与隐私

  • 不用于一次性解释、翻译或内容发布
  • 云端走 secret Gist：未公开列出，但并不等于加密；涉及敏感内容它会先问你
  • 本地模式只写你指定的那个文件，不自己挑路径

Capabilities

What a session looks like

  • You say: “A headline says chip exports are restricted again and GPU prices are climbing — I want to understand the chain.”
  • The side surface grows its skeleton in place —
  • Physics: leading-edge nodes need EUV lithography, and the light sources, mirrors and photoresists come from a handful of suppliers
  • Industry: design → foundry → packaging → channel, each stage on its own capacity and inventory cycle
  • Rules: export lists draw the line by compute density, not by product name
  • Open question: is this rise capacity or channel hoarding? Compare whether old and new cards move together, and whether lead times stretch in step
  • It replies in one or two sentences: “Are old and new cards rising together, or only the new ones? Only the new ones point at capacity and the lists; everything rising looks more like hoarding.”
  • You can watch that document: a native side panel, a local Markdown file you name, or one unlisted Gist in the cloud

When it earns its place

  • You need to understand an unfamiliar field or a new thing, not look up one fact
  • A question keeps resisting you and needs to be asked down, layer by layer
  • You want to co-build an understanding over many turns, not collect one-shot answers
  • You want the hypotheses, counterexamples and confirmed details to survive the conversation
  • It only starts when you ask for it; an ordinary question won't turn into this

How it differs from ordinary chat

  • Chat stays short: at most one causal question per turn, no wall of summary
  • The side document is rewritten in place: never a changelog, and confirmed detail or open uncertainty is never dropped
  • It enters through a concrete phenomenon, digs down to first principles, and marks causal edges that cross layers

Boundaries and privacy

  • Not for one-off explanations, translation or publishing
  • The cloud path uses a secret Gist: unlisted, which is not the same as encrypted — it asks first before publishing anything sensitive
  • Local mode writes only the file you name, and never picks a path itself
