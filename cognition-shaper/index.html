认知塑形

Cognition Shaper

用短促的苏格拉底对话推动思考，同时在侧边维护一份持续生长、无损展开的认知框架。

Build a living cognitive model beside concise Socratic dialogue, expanding ideas in place without losing confirmed detail.

能力

双表面共建

  • 聊天保持短促、具体，每轮最多推进一个因果问题
  • Living Surface 承载完整模型，随对话原位展开而不是记录流水账
  • 适配原生侧边文档、本地 Markdown 与云端远程文档三类环境

认知方法

  • 先锁定使用者、目标、场景与现实约束，再构建框架
  • 从微观现象切入，向下寻找第一性原理底座并标记跨层因果
  • 保留可证伪假设、反例、具体案例与所有已确认细节

适用与边界

  • 适用：明确要求认知塑形或多轮共建持续生长的认知模型
  • 不适用：普通解释、一次性分析、翻译或内容发布
  • 云端默认使用 secret Gist；它仅未公开列出，并非私密存储，失败时回退为聊天全文

Capabilities

Two coordinated surfaces

  • Keeps chat concise and concrete, advancing at most one causal question per turn
  • Maintains the full model in a Living Surface that expands in place instead of becoming a transcript
  • Adapts to native side documents, local Markdown, and remote cloud documents

Cognitive method

  • Locks the user, goal, scene, and real constraints before building the framework
  • Enters through a micro-phenomenon, then finds first-principles bedrock and cross-layer causality
  • Preserves falsifiable hypotheses, counterexamples, concrete cases, and every confirmed detail

Fit and boundaries

  • Use for explicit Cognition Shaper requests or multi-turn co-building of a living cognitive model
  • Not for ordinary explanations, one-shot analysis, translation, or publishing
  • Cloud mode defaults to a secret Gist; it is unlisted, not private, with full-document chat fallback
